00 / Statement Artist Statement

A line that never stops moving cannot repeat — it can only return, shifted.

I am an Iranian-born artist working at the intersection of classical textile logic and generative systems. My work begins with a simple observation: the woven surface — repetitive, mathematically structured, infinitely variable — has always been a form of computation, long before computers.

Spectrum Dance is a chain of oscillating links. Each link is a control point that moves along its own sine wave — its own speed, its own amplitude, its own phase. The chain traces a single smooth curve, and the curve's colour drifts through the spectrum as the frame advances.

Where most generative systems produce a still composition, Spectrum Dance is fundamentally a performance — a dance between control points that never repeats. Each composition captures the dance at a distinct moment, rendered as a live animation that continues to shift. Each piece is defined by a numeric seed and can be regenerated, documented, and adapted for textile, print, and surface applications.

On AI tools — where I encountered technical obstacles, I turned to AI tools for debugging and code optimization. Every structural, aesthetic, and conceptual decision remained my own.

01 / Seed

Variation

A numeric seed initializes the number of control points (2–8), the speed and direction of every link, the length of every link, the initial phase of every sine wave, the starting RGB colour, and the RGB drift range. Each output is regenerated and documented as a distinct, traceable work.

02 / Oscillating

Geometry

Every link is a control point that moves along its own sine wave. The chain traces a smooth curve, and the stroke colour drifts through the spectrum one frame at a time. The animation is live — the curve never stops moving.

03 / Surface

Application

The resulting composition becomes a surface-design asset — explored across garments, textile prints, wallpaper and other pattern-driven applications.
